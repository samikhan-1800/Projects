Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I create an event?

Creating an event is simple! Just register as an organizer, log in to your dashboard, and click "Create New Event". Fill out the event details, set your ticket prices, and submit for approval. Once approved, your event will be live on the platform.

How do I book tickets for an event?

Browse our events page, find an event you like, and click "View Details". On the event page, select the number of tickets you want, fill out your information, and proceed to payment. You'll receive a confirmation email with your tickets.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All transactions are processed securely through our encrypted payment system.

Can I get a refund for my tickets?

Refund policies vary by event and are set by the event organizer. You can find the specific refund policy on each event's detail page. Generally, tickets are refundable up to 48 hours before the event, unless otherwise specified.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach our customer support team through this contact form, by email at support@eventhub.com, or by phone at +1 (555) 123-4567 during business hours. We typically respond to emails within 24 hours.
